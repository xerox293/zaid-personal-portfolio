How I think about business

I've always been intrigued by how businesses work: how different functions, people and processes come together to create something structured, scalable and meaningful. An idea, understood and executed well, can introduce something new, influence an industry and grow far beyond its original vision.

For me, building a product means learning about much more than the product itself: understanding people and their behaviour, what shapes their decisions, and how psychology, advertising, sales, design and operations connect. I'm drawn to that whole process, the different disciplines it brings together, the problems it asks you to solve and the constant opportunity to learn something new.
